# Master's studies in cognitive science

*I graduated with a cognitive science master's degree from the University of Jyväskylä in June of 2023.*

I completed courses in user psychology and research, user experience and interface design, usability and accessibility, project management, and web and mobile development. My master's thesis was on implementing cognitive load in instructional design with a focus on programming learning.

My graduate studies gave me a strong theoretical basis for understanding and considering people as users of digital products. I also grew my expertise in front-end programming with courses from Helsinki, Aalto, and LUT Universities which gave me even better skills in creating usable interfaces.
