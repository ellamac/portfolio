# iPad Guestbook-app

*I wanted to design an app that I could use as a fun guestbook and bulletin board with my old iPad in the entryway.*

I came up with an app that guests could take pics with and I could check my schedule and lists quickly.

![Wireframing the main view](/portfolio/imgs/guestbook-wf1.jpg)
*Wireframing the main view*

I started by brainstorming what I needed to have in the app. I like to write and sketch stuff out by hand. The result is not the prettiest but when working alone it works.

I did wireframing with Figma. The complete wireframing ended up being so high-fidelity that the prototyping and building the rest of the views was really quick. The complete Figma prototype is here.

This project is a work in progress. I yet have to begin programming the actual application.
