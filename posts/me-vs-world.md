# Open source data project

*I took a software development group project course in 2020 where each group made an app utilizing open source data. I focused on the frontend and design of our Me vs the World app that users can use to compare their salaries, electricity usage and educational years to the world average.*

For the course, all participants were divided into random groups of four. We worked on the application for one period during which we also focused on understanding the software development process and group dynamics.

![A screenshot of the app shows how my educational years compare to others in Finland and the world.](/portfolio/imgs/mevsworld2.jpg)
*A screenshot of the app shows how my educational years compare to others in Finland and the world.*

My area was mainly designing and coding the styling of the site. We used React, Node, Google Charts and open data provided by World Bank.

I am happy with the outcome even after a few years. I think the images blending into the black background give a serious and impressive effect for the application that is meant for users to notice their privileges.

The project can be found on [Github](https://github.com/ellamac/Me-vs-the-world--projektikurssi).
