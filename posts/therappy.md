# Flutter group project

*TherAppy is a prototype for a physical therapy app made as a part of LUT University's User Interfaces and Usability course.*

The course covered thoroughly the theory and practice of web and mobile interface design principles, HCI design process, research methods, prototyping, storyboarding and usability as well as implementation with Dart and Flutter.

![Screenshot of the progress view.](/portfolio/imgs/therappy.jpg)
*Screenshot of the progress view.*

My job in our group was to create the Flutter prototype with my group members. I referenced the design that my group member made in Figma and implemented design revisions from the group. As a part of the course, I also reviewed other groups' projects and evaluated them with HCI heuristics. We also completed usability testing for our own application.

The project is a native prototype of a therapy interface for motion-detected interactive guided therapy. The interface represents how the user would interact with the motion detection equipment and projection from their phone. The user can also communicate with their therapist and engage with a community of other patients and their family members. As the user completes therapy sessions, their avatar evolves, adding a gamification element.

The native prototype includes the progress and community views. The complete prototype can be found from <https://github.com/ellamac/therappy>.
