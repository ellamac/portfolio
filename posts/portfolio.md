# Creating my portfolio

*After graduating, I needed some projects to keep up my programming skills and develop my UI/UX proficiency further. But I also needed to display my work somewhere... So I decided to feed two birds with one scone and have my first project be creating my own portfolio site!*

## My process

- Research on other portfolio sites.
- Understanding users
- Brainstorming ideas and solutions
- Sketching by hand
- Wireframing on Figma
- Prototyping on Figma
- Implementing with React

## Redesign

After having the site up for a few months, I decided to do some redesigning. Design was done on Figma and the site updated on October 21st. Also accessibility and some bugs were fixed.
